How to Use the Portal

A step-by-step guide on navigating the Return to Work platform for Employees, Supervisors, and the Company Clinic.

For Employees

1

Filing a "Return to Work" Request

When you are ready to return to work after an absence, you must file a digital request before resuming your duties.

  1. Log In: Access the portal and log in using your Biometrics ID credentials.
  2. Dashboard: Upon logging in, you will be directed to your dashboard where you can see the application form.
  3. Fill Details: Provide the First Date of Absence and your expected Date of Return. The total days of absence will be calculated automatically.
  4. Provide Reason: State your reason clearly.
  5. Supervisor Info: Select whether you notified your superior. If "Yes", type their name to search and select them from the dropdown list.
  6. Upload Certificate: If your absence was due to sickness (except exclusions like Dysmenorrhea), you must click "Upload File" to attach a clear photo or PDF of your Medical Certificate.
  7. Submit: Review your details and click Submit Application.
2

Tracking Your Application Status

You can monitor the progress of your submission under the "Application History" section in your sidebar menu.

Pending

Your supervisor has not yet reviewed your application. Please wait for their approval.

Approved

Your supervisor approved your request. Proceed to the clinic for your Nurse Clearance.

Declined

Your application was rejected. Click "View Details" to read the reason provided by your supervisor or the nurse.

For Approvers (Supervisors/Managers)

A

Reviewing Applications

As a supervisor, you are responsible for authorizing the return of employees under your department.

  1. Dashboard Overview: Your dashboard shows a summary of Pending, Approved, and Declined requests from your department.
  2. Review Pending Items: Click on the "Pending" shortcut card or navigate to the "Pending Actions" tab to view employees waiting for approval.
  3. Inspect Details: Expand an application to review their Reason, Absence Dates, and view their uploaded Medical Certificate.
  4. Take Action:
    • Click Approve if the details are valid. It will be forwarded to the Clinic.
    • Click Decline if there are issues. You must provide a valid "Decline Reason" which the employee will be able to read.

For Company Nurses / Clinic

C

Providing Health Clearance

Nurses perform the final assessment for employees whose requests have already been approved by their supervisor.

  1. New Applications: Navigate to the "New Applications" tab to see a queue of employees arriving at the clinic.
  2. Face-to-Face Assessment: Review the employee's system record against their physical presentation and attached medical certificate.
  3. Issue Declaration: For each employee, select one of the following health declarations:
    Fit to Work
    Cleared to resume all normal duties.
    Return to Work
    Cleared, but with specific limitations (requires remarks).
    Unfit to Work
    Not cleared. Must return home/continue rest.
  4. Remarks: Add any necessary medical remarks or restrictions before saving the declaration.
  5. History: You can review past clearances in the "Declaration History" tab.
